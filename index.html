    Wherebird

    Ten real bird sightings, new every day. Pin where each photo was taken and guess the month.

    Each bird is worth up to 7,000 points for the place and 3,000 for the month. You don't need to know the species: habitat, light, snow, plumage and the bird's company all give things away. When you're stuck, you have five hints to spend across the whole day.

    Photos and records come from iNaturalist observers under Creative Commons licences, credited on every photo. Field notes come from AVONET (Tobias et al. 2022, Ecology Letters). Everyone gets the same birds on the same day.
